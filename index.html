Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of equipment is available?

We carry everything from dumbbells and kettlebells to treadmills, rowing machines, squat racks, and more. Our catalog covers cardio, strength, mobility, and recovery.

How does swapping work?

Request a swap through your account. We schedule a pickup and delivery at the same time so you're never without equipment. Swaps are included in your plan.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime and we'll pick up your equipment within 5 business days at no cost.

What if something breaks?

Normal wear and tear is fully covered. We'll repair or replace any equipment that isn't performing at its best, free of charge.

Where do you deliver?

We currently serve major metro areas across the US. Enter your zip code when signing up to check availability in your area.
