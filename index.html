Frequently Asked Questions

What peptides do you offer?

We carry a wide range of research peptides including BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, CJC-1295, Ipamorelin, and many more. Our catalog is regularly updated with new compounds based on member demand.

How is purity verified?

Every batch is tested by independent third-party laboratories using HPLC and mass spectrometry. Results are published on the certificate of analysis that ships with your order.

Can I change my selections each month?

Absolutely. You can swap peptides, adjust quantities, or change your entire selection before each monthly shipment through your account dashboard.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. All plans are month-to-month. You can pause, modify, or cancel your subscription at any time with no penalties or fees.

How are peptides shipped?

All orders ship in insulated, temperature-controlled packaging with ice packs to maintain stability. Tracking is provided with every shipment. We ship across the US.
